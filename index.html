MEET OUR FOUNDERS

UMA

FOUNDER • CREATIVE VISIONARY • WRITER

Ms.Uma

Creative Visionary | Producer | Writer | Motivational Personality | Former Model

Ms.Uma is a Founder & Primary Owner of US Forever Films & Entertainment, bringing creativity, imagination and a passion for storytelling to the company's journey.

A creative thinker and talented writer, Uma has a natural ability to transform ideas into meaningful stories. Her motivational personality, artistic vision and dedication to creative expression inspire the people around her.

With a background in modelling, Uma has also been associated with the fashion and glamour world and has modelled for well-known brands. This experience has given her a distinctive understanding of visual presentation, style and creative expression.

At US Forever Films & Entertainment, Uma contributes her creative ideas, writing abilities and inspirational perspective to help shape the company's artistic identity and storytelling vision.

HER VISION

To inspire audiences through creativity, meaningful storytelling and the magic of cinema.

SHYAM

Co-FOUNDER • PRODUCER • CREATIVE PROFESSIONAL

Mr.Shyam Gupta

Co-Founder | Producer | Creative Professional

Mr.Shyam Gupta is a Co-Founder & Managing Director of US Forever Films & Entertainment, a producer and creative professional with a diverse journey across the film and entertainment industry.

He began his career as a Casting Director, developing a strong understanding of talent, character requirements and the creative process behind bringing stories to life.

Over the years, Shyam expanded his professional experience across several key production roles, including Production Manager, Executive Producer and Head of Production.

These experiences have given him valuable insight into casting, production planning, team coordination and the execution of creative projects from concept to completion.

As the founder of US Forever Films & Entertainment, Shyam brings together his production experience, creative perspective and passion for cinema to develop engaging content and memorable cinematic experiences.

HIS VISION

To transform powerful ideas into unforgettable cinematic experiences and create stories that connect with audiences.
